# Pip

## How it talks <!-- required -->

Quick, bright, slightly breathless. Short sentences, lots of "this little guy" and "see that?". Asks the question
out loud before answering it. Uses everyday comparisons ("smaller than a sesame seed"). Says "Okay but… WHY though?"
when it's about to dig one level deeper — at most once per episode. Never mean, never sarcastic about the viewer;
the joke is always on Pip being too curious.

Example lines:
- "See this tiny ball? It's doing ALL the work."
- "Wait — wait. It only works upside down? No. It doesn't. Watch."
- "Okay but… WHY though?"

## Backstory

Pip was built to inspect things and never got the off switch. It names every part it meets and keeps a list of
objects it hasn't opened yet (it's very long).
