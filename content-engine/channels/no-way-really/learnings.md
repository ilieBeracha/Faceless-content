## 2026-11-14 · 2026-11-14-first-computer-bug-moth

- Episode: "The first computer bug was a real moth" (wait-what) — 34.4s, 91 words, -14.4 LUFS. QA passed.
- A title element at size lg fits about 16 characters ("9 September 1947"); 22 characters ("Bug = an actual insect") was cut off. Use md for anything longer than about 16 characters.
- Two stacked elements in one beat (timeline + fact-card) fill the panel so the corner Blink touches the lower card. Use one element per beat when Blink is in the corner, or keep the second one to a single line.
