## 2026-10-15 · 2026-10-15-a-billion-seconds

- Episode: "How long is a billion seconds?" (bigger-than-you-think) — 45.5s, 119 words, -15.0 LUFS. QA passed.
- One scene per sentence with the number on a sign works: viewers see each figure as it is said — keep it.
- Short first sentence ('A billion seconds.') lands the hook at ~1.3 s; reuse that pattern.
- Use 'surprised' or 'curious' rather than 'worried'/'thinking' for Dot when the line is wonder, not concern — those brows read as cross at phone size.
