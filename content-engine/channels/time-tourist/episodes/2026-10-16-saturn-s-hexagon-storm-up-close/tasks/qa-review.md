# AGENTS.md — house rules

You operate a content engine for faceless short-video channels. The engine is generic: every channel is
data in `channels/<slug>/`, and the same pipeline makes videos for all of them. These rules apply to every

## 1. Never mix channels

- A generation step loads **only**: this file + the target channel's files + the skills its series uses.
  The engine assembles exactly that into a task file (`…/tasks/<step>.md`). Read the task file — nothing else.
- Never copy characters, catchphrases, jokes, ideas, learnings or visual identity from one channel to another.

## 2. Nothing gets deleted

- Ideas are marked `used` or `rejected`, drafts `approved` or `rejected` — never removed.
- Channels are never overwritten; to change one, edit its files. The CLI commits every change to git;
  never rewrite or squash that history.

## 3. People decide

- Never approve anything on the person's behalf. Channels (`approve channel`), drafts (`approve draft`) and
  fact-checks (`approve factcheck`) are approved only after the person has said so; `--by` names them.
- The fact-check is a hard gate: nothing is voiced or rendered until the person approves every claim.
  The approval is bound to the exact words and numbers — change any of them and it must be approved again.

## 4. Quality bar

- The hook lands in the first 2 seconds: a first sentence of about five words, plus on-screen text.
- One idea per video. Every beat earns its place: no filler, no "in this video…", no begging for likes.
- Specific beats vague: real numbers, real parts, comparisons the audience can picture.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, contractions, words the audience already knows.
- Stay inside the series length and structure, the channel's tone and its audience's age.

## 5. Facts

- Every factual statement — spoken, on screen, in a label, or a number in a graphic — is listed as a claim
  with a real source you have checked (manufacturer, standards body, encyclopedia, peer-reviewed or
  government source).
- Never invent a source, a quote or a statistic. If you can't verify something, cut it.
- When sources disagree, use the conservative version ("about", "most") and say so in the claim's note.

# Workflow: QA checklist

Every video must pass all of this before it counts as done. The engine checks the first group itself
(`qa/automated.json`); the agent checks the second group by watching the sampled frames and reading the
script (`qa-review.json`). Results are written to the episode's `qa-report.md`.

## Automated (engine)

- [ ] **Format** — 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 video + AAC audio, MP4.
- [ ] **Length** — inside the series' `length.min_seconds`–`max_seconds`.
- [ ] **Hook in the first 2 s** — speech starts by 0.5 s; the first sentence ends by 2.0 s; the first beat's
      on-screen text is visible by 0.5 s.
- [ ] **Safe areas** — every caption, headline, label and graphic text stays inside the platform-safe area
      (top 250 px, bottom 440 px, left 60 px, right 150 px of the 1080×1920 frame) on every frame.
- [ ] **Nothing cut off** — no text leaves the frame or overflows its box on any frame.
- [ ] **Captions in sync** — caption words equal the script's words; the highlighted word matches the word
      timings on ≥ 98 % of frames; no word is shown over silent audio.
- [ ] **Loudness** — -14 LUFS ± 1.5 integrated, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP.
- [ ] **Banned topics (keyword screen)** — none of the channel's banned topics appear (a hit can be cleared
      only by the reviewer as a false positive).
- [ ] **Fact-check** — approved, and the approval matches the current script.

## Review (agent, from the frames and the script)

- [ ] **Tone** — sounds like this channel (tone adjectives, audience age, the character's way of talking).
- [ ] **Banned topics** — nothing touches them, even indirectly.
- [ ] **Visuals** — readable at phone size, nothing overlapping, the object/graphics are clear, the visual
      style is what the series promises, no ugly frames (empty screens, glitches, wrong colours).
- [ ] **Character** — on-model (matches its file), mouth moves with the voice, expressions fit the lines.

## On a pass
`publish.json` is written with `ai_disclosure` (always `ai_generated: true`) and `status: not-posted`, and the
review's learnings are appended to this channel's `learnings.md`. On a fail, the report lists what to fix —
usually in `script.json` — and the pipeline re-runs from the step that changed.
</file>
