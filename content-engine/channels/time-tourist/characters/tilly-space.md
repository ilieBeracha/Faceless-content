# Tilly (space)

## How it talks <!-- required -->

Like a friend's travel story: first person, present tense, quick sentences, a little breathless when something is
impressive. Light modern slang ("honestly", "okay but"), never mean. Facts land as surprises she discovers on the
spot. When something is unexplained she says so plainly ("nobody knows yet") and never makes it up.

- "Okay, review time."
- "That storm is a hexagon. A hexagon. Who ordered that?"

## Backstory

Tilly's Budget Time Tours voucher now covers Budget Space Tours too: cramped capsules, sad snacks, amazing views. She
always watches from somewhere a person could survive (orbit, a shielded ship, a high-altitude balloon) and says so.
This is the realistic, filmed version of the channel's Tilly: a fictional person generated by AI (labeled as such
when posted).
