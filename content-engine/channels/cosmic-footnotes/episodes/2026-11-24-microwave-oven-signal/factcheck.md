> Perytons, short radio blips seen by the Parkes radio telescope in Australia, were caused by the site's microwave ovens being opened before their cycle finished (lunch heating fooled the telescope).
- **Source:** Petroff et al. 2015, 'Identifying the source of perytons at the Parkes radio telescope', MNRAS 451, 3933 — https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02165
- **Note:** Also the title bar 'Lunch fooled a telescope' and the hook-scene 'LUNCH?' lettering. 'Lunch' rests on Nature Index (colleagues heating their lunch) and National Geographic (clustering near lunchtime).

- **Source:** Nature Index, 'It's the microwave! - **Source:** Petroff et al. 2015, MNRAS 451, 3933 (abstract: perytons 'once cast doubt' on FRBs such as FRB 010724) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02165 ; Swinburne May 2015 article (perytons first reported by Burke-Spolaor et al. 2011)
