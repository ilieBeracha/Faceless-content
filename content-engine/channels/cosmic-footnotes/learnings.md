## 2026-10-15 · 2026-10-15-gps-needs-einstein

- Episode: "Your phone needs Einstein" (tiny-clue-big-deal) — 45.2s, 130 words, -15.2 LUFS. QA passed.
- The 'point' beat works best as a consequence for the viewer ('your map would drift…'), not as 'this changed science'.
- On night/space scenes, put numbers on a sign — hand-lettered text is low-contrast on dark skies.
- Very short sentences (e.g. 'That sounds like nothing.') get a scene for under a second; merge them into the next sentence's scene.
