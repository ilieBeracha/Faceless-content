slug: big-numbers
name: Big Numbers, Tiny Stories
niche: Mind-bending numbers (factorials, billions, light-years, odds) made graspable through short illustrated stories with one stick figure.
audience: Teens and adults who love "wait, that can't be true" facts about maths, time and scale
why_it_works: >-
  Huge-number shorts are a proven format: each one is a single mind-blowing claim plus a step-by-step visual metaphor
  ("if you took one step every billion years…"). The stories are endlessly repeatable (time, money, probability,
  space, population), each sentence is one drawable scene, and maths facts are easy to verify precisely.
character: >-
  Dot, an original round-headed doodle person — a big beige circle head with dot eyes and a three-hair tuft, stick
  limbs, endlessly patient (stick-figure rig). Dot acts out every metaphor: piling rice, walking the equator,
  emptying oceans one drop at a time. Calm, warm narrator voice (Kokoro bm_george or af_heart).
series: >-
  "Bigger Than You Think" (40–55 s): the claim (hook) → why it's huge → the story (3–5 escalating scenes) → the
  twist → one-line callback. Beats: hook 3 s, setup 8 s, story 25 s, twist 8 s, kicker 4 s.
visual_style: >-
  Storybook illustrations in muted watercolour tones on paper (sage hills, sand, slate sea, beige sky), hand-drawn
  wobbly lines, a new scene for every sentence, a two-tone title bar on top, tiny one-word captions in the middle
  of the picture.
render_skills: [render-storybook]
sample_ideas:
  - A shuffled deck of cards is (almost certainly) a brand-new order
  - How long is a billion seconds?
  - How many grains of rice would 64 doublings take?
  - How long would it take to count to a million?
risks: >-
  The genre is popular, so the character and stories must stay original (no copied scripts or scenes). Numbers need
  exact maths and careful rounding. Free code-drawn scenes are simpler than AI illustrations; AI images would cost
  money per video.

## Pitch

Every huge number becomes one tiny story Dot can walk you through.
